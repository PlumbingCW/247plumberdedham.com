Most faucet problems are a drip you can live with for a day. A few are not: a valve that will not shut off, a split supply line, or a shower valve leaking inside a wall. For those, close your main shutoff, then call our 24/7 emergency line at (617) 752-0573.

Valve will not shut off or a supply line is leaking right now.

This is the situation that sends people to the phone at 2 a.m. Every fixture should have its own stop valve. When the valve turns and the water stops, you have bought time. When the valve spins freely, leaks at the stem, or will not move, you need the main shutoff and a plumber.

Closing the main valve is the right call whether the line is a burst flexible supply or a faucet body that cracked. Then open the lowest faucet in the house to drain pressure, and put a bucket under the damage. If the trouble is out at the street or the meter, the DWWD after-hours water emergency line is 781-326-1250. For a burst pipe inside the walls, see burst pipe repair.

How we find the real fault.

A plumber starts by turning the fixture off and on and watching where water appears. A drip at the spout points to the cartridge or seat. Water around the base of the faucet points to O-rings or a cracked body.

Water below the sink points to supply lines, stop valves or the drain. We pull the cartridge and look at the seat before quoting, because a pitted seat means the cartridge alone will not cure the drip.

A plumber cannot see inside a plaster or tile wall, so a shower valve leak sometimes needs a small access opening. Ask whether the plumber will test for pressure loss before cutting, and whether the opening will be sized for a patch. Slow wall leaks that continue after the valve is fixed may need leak detection.

Old homes, hard water and what wears out first.

About 74 percent of Dedham houses were built before 1978, according to the Town's Master Plan. Fixtures from several decades of remodels often sit side by side with original stop valves that have not been turned in years. Those valves are the weak link. A seized valve is the usual reason a simple faucet swap becomes a longer job, and replacing it is the cheapest time to do it.

DWWD reports average hardness around 6.0 grains per gallon in 2025, from 4.3 to 7.8 across samples. That is enough to crust aerators and slow cartridge movement. If your faucet sputters or the handle feels gritty, the first test is cleaning the aerator and checking the cartridge for scale.

DWWD also says lead in water comes mainly from service lines and home plumbing, so if you are replacing old fixtures, ask the plumber to note any old lines they see.

Cold weather and outdoor spigots.

The Boston-area January normal low is 23.1 F (NOAA 1991-2020, Boston Logan), so hose bibs freeze every winter. A frozen spigot can crack inside the wall and only show itself in spring. Disconnect the hose by October, and shut and drain the interior stop valve if the house has one.

DWWD recommends a vacuum breaker on hose connections, and the state code asks for backflow protection on sillcocks. If a spigot drips inside after a freeze, call us before you turn it on again.

Repair, replace or upgrade: how to decide.

Repair makes sense when the faucet is under about ten years old, the body is clean, and a cartridge or washer fixes the leak. Replacement is better when the finish is flaking, parts are no longer sold, or you have repaired the same faucet twice. Replacing a faucet is a fixture replacement, and the Massachusetts plumbers fact sheet says that work can need a permit. A leaky-faucet repair does not.

Three questions to ask on the phone:

  1. Does the quote include new supply lines and a new stop valve?.
  2. Will the plumber test the shutoff before leaving?.
  3. Who supplies the faucet, and what warranty does the maker offer?.

Tubs, showers and drains that go with it.

Shower valve leaks and tub faucet drips are the same family of repair, only the cartridge is behind a trim plate. If the water is slow to leave the tub or shower floor, the drain may be partly blocked with hair and soap. We clear that during the same visit when needed. If clogs return, look at drain cleaning as the longer fix.
