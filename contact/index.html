What to have ready when you call.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street or landmark.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have already shut off the main water valve.
  • Whether anyone is at risk, and whether water is near electrical equipment.
  • Whether you own the home, rent it or manage it, so the work is booked correctly.

Water, gas and sewer emergencies that are not ours to fix.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 from outside, then call your gas utility. For a water main problem in the street, call the Dedham-Westwood Water District at 781-329-7090 during office hours or 781-326-1250 after hours. For a sewer problem in the street, contact the Town of Dedham. Problems inside your home are what the plumber handles. See plumbing services or service areas for more.
