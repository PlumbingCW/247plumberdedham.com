Riverdale is the part of Dedham most closely tied to the Charles River. The Town Clerk describes it as "completely surrounded" by the river and reached by crossing several bridges. That geography makes water the main plumbing topic here, though the records do not say that any particular house has a water problem.

Call (617) 752-0573 if water is already where it should not be.

What Riverdale is, from the Town's records.

Dedham's master plan lists Riverdale as one of six neighborhoods. It covers about 1,064 acres and roughly 1,560 housing units, which works out to about 1.5 homes per acre, the second lowest density in town after West Dedham. The Town Clerk says it was farmland and has a lot of waterfront property.

The plan also describes the Long Ditch, a canal dug in the 1650s, crossing the northern edge of the peninsula. Riverdale School and a Town parcel next to Cutler Park, a state reservation shared with Needham, Newton and Boston, are named as local features. The plan publishes no boundary line, so this page does not draw one.

The flood plain, in plain terms.

About 19 percent of Dedham's land lies in the Town's Flood Plain Overlay District, and the master plan says the largest portion is in Riverdale. That is a zoning overlay based on federal flood maps. It tells you where building rules are stricter. It does not tell you whether your own basement has taken on water.

To see how a specific address is mapped, ask the Town's building or conservation staff instead of guessing from the neighborhood name. Ask your insurer separately what your policy covers, since flood and sewer backup are often handled differently from a standard homeowners policy.

Sump pumps, basements and sewer backups.

  • Test the pump before heavy rain by pouring water into the pit until the float lifts it..
  • Check that the discharge line goes outside and is not frozen or blocked..
  • Consider a battery backup for power outages, which usually come with storms..
  • Never route a sump pump or roof drain into the sanitary sewer. The Town says clean water in the sewer is illegal..
  • If a floor drain rises during a storm, stop running water and call..

Other plumbing help in Riverdale.

A sewer camera inspection shows the real condition of a line after a backup or before you buy. Leak detection finds a hidden supply leak when the meter keeps moving with everything off. Sewer line cleaning clears a main that backs up to the lowest drain.

What to do while you wait.

Shut off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel from dry ground. Stay out of standing water, keep children and pets away from sewage, and photograph the damage. Codes, standards and sources.

Plumbing work in Massachusetts is governed by 248 CMR 10.00, the Uniform State Plumbing Code, and plumbers are licensed by the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters. You can read the state's consumer fact sheet on plumbers and gas fitters before you hire anyone.
