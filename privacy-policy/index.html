Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Dedham 24/7 Plumbing operates this website and a 24/7 phone line. This policy is short because the site is simple: it contains no forms and no accounts, and people reach us by phone.

Information we receive.

When you call, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell the dispatcher, such as your address and a description of the problem. We use it to book and carry out the service you asked for. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start.

Information from the website.

This website does not collect personal information through forms. Your browser may send standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type to our hosting provider when pages load. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map, which may receive technical data from your browser under Google's own policies.

How we use and share information.

We use call details to dispatch a plumber, provide the service, follow up on the job and improve our service. We share the details a plumber needs to reach you and do the work. We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping and deleting information.

We keep call records only as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal or accounting reasons. To ask about your information, email contact@247plumberdedham.com.

Changes.

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can be sent to the email above or answered by phone at (617) 752-0573. Back to the homepage.

Cookies and analytics.

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add basic analytics to see which pages are used, the tool will record page views and general device information, not names or phone numbers. Your browser settings let you block or clear cookies at any time.

Your choices.

You can ask us to delete the details we hold from your call, and you can ask which details those are. We will answer by email or phone. Because the website has no forms and no accounts, there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
