A garbage disposal is a motor and a grinding plate under the kitchen sink that shreds food scraps so they flush down the drain.

Emergency garbage disposal repair in Dedham, MA covers jams, dead motors, leaks and the kitchen clogs that come with them.

Never put your hand in the opening, even when the power is off. Use tongs or pliers.

Sink about to overflow and the disposal will not move.

Kitchen emergencies cluster around holidays, when a house full of guests means peels, rice and fat all go down one drain. The disposal stops, the sink fills, and the dishwasher drain may back into the basin too. Do not run the dishwasher again until the sink is clear.

Three quick checks come before a service call:

  1. Is the unit on? Press the red reset button on the bottom of the disposal..
  2. Does it hum without turning? Switch it off, then turn the hex socket on the underside with an Allen key to free a jam..
  3. Is water standing in the sink? That is usually a clog in the trap or the line beyond the disposal, not the motor..

If these checks fail, call. A plumber can tell in minutes whether the blockage is in the disposal or in the drain after it.

Symptoms and what each one means.

Hums but does not spin.

A jam. Something hard, such as a bone, fruit pit or bottle cap, is wedged between the impellers and the plate. Power off, free it with the hex key, press reset and test. If the hum returns, the motor or start capacitor may be failing.

Nothing happens when you flip the switch.

Check the reset button, the breaker, and the outlet under the sink. A dead unit with a good circuit usually means a burned motor, and replacement beats repair.

Water drips from underneath.

Find the source with a dry paper towel. A leak at the top flange is a failed seal. A leak at the dishwasher hose or drain pipe is a loose clamp. A leak from the bottom of the casing means the housing has cracked, and the unit must be replaced.

A disposal sends more solids into that route than a strainer basket does. If your sink clogs even when the unit is running well, the line is the problem, and drain cleaning deals with it.

Fat is the biggest troublemaker. It looks like liquid in the pan and then sets inside cool pipe. The Town of Dedham maintains the public sanitary sewer, and grease that leaves your pipes can add to blockages further down the line. Wipe pans with a paper towel and put the towel in the trash.

Hard water and the grinding chamber.

DWWD's 2025 water quality report gives an average of about 6.0 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard. Mineral scale can build on the flange, the stopper and the inside of the chamber, so a disposal that smells or grinds slowly may be scale and old residue.

Run cold water with the unit on, then grind a handful of ice and a halved lemon. If it still labors, call, because the seal or the bearings may be wearing.

Installing a new disposal.

A new unit needs a clean sink flange, a proper connection to the trap, an air gap or high loop for the dishwasher hose, and a safe electrical connection. Swapping a disposal can involve the drain and the water supply, so the plumbing work goes through a licensed plumber, and the Massachusetts fact sheet says replacing fixtures can require a permit.

If your trap has been dripping slowly for months, we replace it at the same visit. Some cabinets hide rot behind the plumbing, and a disposal swap is the moment to find it.
