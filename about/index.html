Dedham 24/7 Plumbing is an emergency plumbing line for Dedham, Massachusetts. We take the call, ask what is happening, and book the next available licensed and insured plumber.

How a call is handled.

A dispatcher answers the phone. You describe the problem and your address, and they ask a few safety questions, such as whether water is near electrical equipment or whether anyone smells gas. They then book the next available plumber. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows. We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because call volume, weather and traffic change it.

What we do not claim.

We do not publish a licence number, because the plumbers who respond hold their own licences, and we do not publish reviews, ratings or a count of jobs done. Massachusetts requires licensed master or journeyman plumbers for plumbing work, so every plumber who comes to your home should be able to show a licence type and number.

How to check any plumber's licence.

  • Ask for the licence type (master or journeyman) and number when the plumber arrives.
  • Look the number up on the state's online licensing portal, run by the Division of Occupational Licensure.
  • Or call the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters, which publishes its phone number on Mass.gov.
  • Ask who pulls the permit if the job is a replacement, since only licensed plumbers can do so in Massachusetts.

Where we work.

We cover Dedham and four nearby towns: Westwood, Needham, Norwood and Walpole. The service areas page explains how each town differs. For a full list of work, see plumbing services.

What you can expect on the phone.

The first minute is about safety and facts. The dispatcher asks where the water is coming from, whether you can reach the shutoff valve and whether anyone is at risk. Those answers decide how urgent the visit is and what the plumber should bring. If the problem can wait for a normal appointment, you are told that plainly instead of being pushed into a premium emergency visit.

How the 24/7 promise is kept honest.

A phone line that claims to be open all night has to be answered all night, so the number is meant to reach a person at any hour. We do not claim an arrival time, because we cannot control traffic, weather or the job before yours.

What we do control is how fast the call is answered and how clearly the problem is described to the plumber before they leave for your address. You can call (617) 752-0573 to test that at any hour.

What we do not do.

We do not take job details through a web form, because a form cannot ask the follow-up questions that matter in an emergency. We do not publish ratings or testimonials that cannot be checked. We do not give out phone prices as a promise, since the real price depends on what the plumber finds. And we do not push replacement when a repair is sensible.

Local knowledge behind the advice.

The guides and service pages draw on public sources: the Dedham-Westwood Water District for water quality, the Town of Dedham for sewer and permit rules, Mass.gov and the state plumbing code for licensing and installation rules. Where a fact could not be confirmed, the page says to ask the District or the Town instead of guessing. Browse the plumbing guides or all services to see how that looks in practice.

The number is (617) 752-0573. The contact page lists what to have ready when you call. We work by phone, with no walk-in counter, so calling first is the quickest way to get an answer about your problem and what happens next.

Questions people ask before they call.

Many callers want to know whether the plumber who arrives is licensed and insured, and the honest answer is that you should ask and check. Others ask whether we work on commercial property, which is covered on the commercial plumbing page.

Some ask whether we handle heating, and boiler faults are covered on the boiler repair page. If your question is not here, the dispatcher can answer it in a minute, and there is never a charge for asking.
