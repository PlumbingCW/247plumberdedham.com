An emergency plumber in Walpole, MA is a licensed plumber who comes out at any hour for water you cannot stop or sewage you cannot hold back. We serve Walpole from our Dedham base, about 10 to 12 miles away by our estimate, with 24/7 dispatch day and night.

Shut the main water valve, keep power off in wet rooms and call. Walpole works as three villages, so this page starts there.

Walpole Center, East Walpole and South Walpole.

Walpole has three villages and three ZIPs: 02081 for Walpole and the center, 02032 for East Walpole and 02071 for South Walpole. Blackburn Hall (dedicated 1932) and Town Hall sit on School Street in the center. East Walpole has the former Bird Company mill complex on the Neponset and the Old Post Road School.

When you call, name the village and the nearest cross street. That gets the right plumber headed to the right address faster than a ZIP alone.

Bird Park, the Town Forest and river lots.

Francis William Bird Park, 89 acres created in 1925 and owned by The Trustees of Reservations since 2002, and the Walpole Town Forest near School Meadow Brook and the Neponset anchor a town full of trees and water. Homes on wooded or river-adjacent lots tend to see more root trouble and damp ground than homes on open streets.

If you have a slow main drain or a damp basement, ask the plumber for a sewer inspection before blaming a fixture. Tell the dispatcher whether you are on town sewer or a private system, since we could not confirm which areas are on which.

Groundwater, treatment and a planned MWRA connection.

The Walpole Sewer and Water Division, part of Public Works, runs the town's water and sewer systems. The water comes from the town's own wells and treatment plants. A third-party data site lists air stripping, filtration and chlorine pre-oxidation as the steps, and we treat that as a secondary source. The Town's Water Quality Reports page is the place to check.

Separately, the Town is planning an MWRA connection. A state environmental notice dated June 13, 2025 describes a pump station on Mylod Street near the Norwood line and about 1,500 feet of main to bring MWRA water through the town of Norwood as a supplemental supply.

Planned is the word, so do not assume it is running. If your water changes in taste or color, ask the Division before assuming a pipe in your house has failed.

The 2026 outdoor water policy.

The Town's Mandatory Water Demand Management Policy took effect June 10, 2026 and runs until further notice. The state directed the Town to restrict outdoor use under its Water Management Permit because of worsening drought. We have not confirmed today's status, so check the Town's current notice before you water or test a sprinkler.

Rule What the policy says.
Lawn watering One day a week: odd addresses Tuesday, even addresses Thursday.
Sprinklers and automatic irrigation Prohibited 9 AM to 5 PM any day.
Exempt Hand-held hose with a spring-loaded nozzle, a watering can, and public parks and fields before 9 AM or after 5 PM.
Enforcement Fines apply, and the Town may escalate to a total ban on non-essential outdoor use.

A zone valve that sticks or a hose bib that drips wastes water with no sprinkler running, so a quick check is worth doing now. Leak detection can find a buried irrigation line that keeps losing pressure.

While you wait: before the plumber reaches your Walpole address.

Close the main and any irrigation valve.

If a sprinkler line or hose bib is the source, shut the irrigation valve too, because a second line can keep feeding the leak. Then close the main where water enters the house.

Keep power off near wet floors.

Whether the water came from a pipe, a failed sump or a heater, stay out of standing water. Unplug what you can reach from dry floor and leave the panel alone if the floor around it is wet.

Hold back the drains.

If a drain gurgles or a toilet rises, stop using water. We cannot tell by phone whether a backup is your line or the Town's, so tell the dispatcher what you see.

Online permits and town rebates.

Walpole's Building Department, Inspectional Services, handles residential and commercial plumbing permits entirely online. Plans, insurance and license are submitted electronically. Inspections are scheduled at (508) 660-7324 and run weekday mornings from 8 to 10 AM, with after-hours and weekend inspections by request for a fee.

The Sewer and Water page also lists rebate applications for toilets and washers and a second-meter irrigation application. If an emergency leads you to replace a toilet, check the rebate page before you buy, and see toilet repair for the repair-or-replace question.

Codes, standards and sources.
